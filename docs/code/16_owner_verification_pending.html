Frequently Asked Questions about Verification

Understand how MoveApp ensures transparency, protects property rights, and keeps Nairobi rental search fraud-free.

Why does MoveApp require property ownership verification? keyboard_arrow_down
To eliminate phantom listings, “Kamagera” rogue brokers, and unauthorized viewing fee extortions across Nairobi. By validating title deeds, county ground lease certificates, or formal agency management agreements, MoveApp guarantees prospective tenants that they are transacting directly with legitimate landlords and authorized caretakers. This leads to 3.2x faster signed agreements and zero deposit escrow disputes.
How long does the verification audit take? keyboard_arrow_down
Standard digital review takes between 12 to 24 business hours. If a physical inspection of the premises or title clarification with the Ministry of Lands ArdhiSasa liaison is necessary, a dedicated verification officer will contact you via WhatsApp or phone to confirm a quick schedule.
Can I create listings while my account is pending verification? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes! You can draft apartment units, upload high-definition photography, configure utility rules (water meter billing, token meters, security fees), and structure payment terms in advance. However, listings remain marked as “Pending Review” and will not show in tenant search results until your landlord badge is active.
What documents qualify as Proof of Property Ownership? keyboard_arrow_down
We accept Certified Freehold Title Deeds, Certificate of Lease, Valid Letters of Allotment backed by current municipal rate clearance, or an officially stamped Notarized Property Management Mandate / Power of Attorney if you are an agent acting on behalf of the registered property owner.
Is my personal data and property deed documentation secure? keyboard_arrow_down
MoveApp is registered under the Kenya Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). Submitted ownership deeds are strictly watermarked with internal verification tokens, never exposed to public inquiries or tenants, and accessible solely to sworn compliance reviewers.
